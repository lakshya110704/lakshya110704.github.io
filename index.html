Inventory: running a steel factory from truck to dispatch

Problem
A steel coil manufacturer tracked material, orders and production across paper and spreadsheets, so it was hard to know what was in stock, in progress or shipped.
What I built
A web app for the client's whole workflow:
  • Logging deliveries at the gate
  • Registering each coil with a printed QR tag
  • Picking coils for customer orders
  • Tracking each production step and recording dispatch
  • A WhatsApp bot that answers customer queries with automated replies
  • A portal for each client to see their order history and status, and reorder on their own
Result
Within one month of on-ground testing, the client reported 60% fewer errors in their work and clearer visibility into their processes.
